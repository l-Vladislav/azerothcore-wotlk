# Fortune Pool — Enchant Guide by Role & Tier

Use Fortune scrolls (Inscription: Glyph of Fortune) on equipment to roll a random
enchant from the Fortune pool. The enchant depends on your item's **type**, **iLvl**,
and **stat profile** (role).

## How it works
1. Craft or buy a Fortune scroll matching your item level range
2. Use on an unenchanted piece of equipment
3. The item gets a random Fortune enchant based on its role and type
4. Use Attribute Recalibrator (41605) to re-roll within the same pool
